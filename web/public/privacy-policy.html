Privacy Policy for Ope

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Ope ("the app", "we", "us") is an operations forecasting and decision tool for small businesses, available as a website and as an Android app. This policy covers both. This policy explains what information Ope collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Ope is operated by an individual developer (contact details at the end). Please read it before using the app.

Who is responsible for your data

Ope is built and operated by an independent solo developer, not a registered company. You can reach the developer using the contact details at the end of this policy for any privacy question or request.

What information we collect

Ope only collects the information needed to provide the service:

  • Account information — your email address and your business name, used to create and secure your account.
  • Business operational data — the information you enter to run forecasts and decisions, such as daily customer counts, sales (including the time of each sale when you use tap-to-record), products and their details, prices and the currency you trade in, stock counts and deliveries, bookings and appointments you record, opening hours, events and advertising periods you mark, and your settings.
  • Information about your regular customers — if you choose to use the regulars feature, names and spending information that you enter about your repeat customers. You are responsible for ensuring you have the right to enter this information (see "Information you enter about other people" below).
  • Feedback you send — if you submit feedback, the name, business, and message you provide. This is emailed to the developer so it can be read and answered.
  • Telegram connection — only if you choose to connect the optional Telegram assistant: the identifier of the Telegram chat you link, so messages can be sent to the right place. You can disconnect it at any time in Settings, which deletes that link.

Ope does not collect more than this. It does not track your location, does not access your contacts, camera, microphone or photos, does not read anything on your device outside the app, contains no advertising or analytics trackers, and does not use your data for advertising profiling. The Android app requests only one permission — internet access — because it has to talk to our server.

How we use your information

Your information is used solely to provide and improve the service: to run forecasts, generate ordering and staffing suggestions, show you insights about your own business, maintain your account, and respond to feedback or support requests. Your business data is used to serve you — it is not sold.

Information you enter about other people

If you record information about your regular customers, you are entering personal data about other people. You are responsible for handling that information lawfully — including having any consent or legal basis required where you operate. You can delete this information at any time within the app.

How your information is stored and who can access it

Your data is stored in a secure cloud database and served through cloud hosting providers that operate the technical infrastructure on our behalf:

  • Supabase — database and authentication.
  • Render — backend application hosting.
  • Vercel — web application hosting.
  • Sentry — error monitoring, which may record technical error details to help fix problems. It is not configured to collect personal information, but an error report can include the address of the page you were on when something went wrong.
  • Telegram — only if you connect the optional Telegram assistant. In that case the messages Ope sends you (for example a forecast, or a reminder to reorder) travel through Telegram’s servers to your chat, and are subject to Telegram’s own privacy policy. If you never connect it, nothing is sent to Telegram.

These providers process data only to run the service and are bound by their own privacy and security terms. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Each business's data is isolated — other users of Ope cannot access your business's data. All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS, and your data is encrypted at rest by the database provider.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete your associated data, except where we are required to keep certain records by law.

Your choices and rights, and deleting your account

You can view, edit, and delete your business data directly within the app, and you can export a copy of it at any time. To delete your account and everything attached to it, email the address at the bottom of this page from the address you signed up with; the account and its data are deleted, and you will get confirmation when that is done. Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU GDPR or Israel's privacy law), you may have additional rights to access, correct, or erase your personal data, which we will honour.

Children

Ope is a tool for businesses and is not intended for use by children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including authenticated access and isolation of each business's data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
